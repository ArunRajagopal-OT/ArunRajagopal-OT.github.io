01 · Executive Profile

OT Cybersecurity Leadership for Critical and Regulated Environments

Engineering reality, cyber risk, regulation and commercial delivery brought together in one operating view.

Arun Rajagopal is a senior OT cybersecurity and industrial cybersecurity expert with more than 25 years of experience across industrial automation, control systems, cybersecurity consulting, architecture, programme delivery and business leadership.

His experience spans PLC, SCADA, DCS and safety systems through to cyber strategy, regulatory assurance, security transformation and executive decision support.

He has held Head of Cyber Security, Managing Principal Consultant, Principal OT Cyber Security Consultant, Security Architect, Delivery Manager and Department Manager responsibilities, alongside commercial ownership covering P&L, recruitment, proposals, client relationships and delivery.

This combination enables him to connect engineering, safety, availability, cyber risk, regulation and business priorities in operational environments.
