Privacy and cookies

Last updated 12 October 2026

This website is the personal portfolio of Arun Rajagopal, based in London, United Kingdom. This note explains what the site measures, why, and the choices you have.

What is measured, and why

The site uses two analytics tools, only to understand how visitors use it so it can be improved:

  • Google Analytics (Google): pages viewed, time on the site, how you arrived (for example from LinkedIn or a search), approximate location (city or country), and device and browser type.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft): how pages are used, such as clicks, scrolling and anonymised replays of visits, so layout problems can be found.

Nothing is used for advertising, nothing is sold, and the site has no forms that collect your details. Google's optional data sharing for its own products is switched off.

Cookies

These cookies are only set if you choose Accept:

If you choose Reject, or make no choice, no analytics cookies are set. Google Analytics then only receives a basic, cookieless signal that a page was viewed, and Clarity records each page view separately without identifying you across pages or visits. Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage under the name ar-consent.
